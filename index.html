Public projects

Sentiment analysis on social media with language models

Feb 2024 to Jun 2024. Master's thesis, Universidad de Sevilla.

This project compares ways to classify the sentiment of tweets as negative, neutral or positive, with the TweetEval dataset. It compares classical models, encoder-only transformers (BERT, DistilBERT, RoBERTa) and decoder-only language models (Phi-3, Mistral 7B). It measures performance, data needs, model size and efficiency.

Decoder-only models reached 0.72 weighted F1 (state of the art: 0.73) with only 5% of the training data. I built the training and fine-tuning pipelines with quantization, sharding and QLoRA, and tracked experiments with MLflow and Optuna. The project also includes an analysis of hashtags with graph modelling, semantic search and clustering.

Transformers, TGI, bitsandbytes, SentenceTransformers, scikit-learn, MLflow, Optuna

14 stars

Early prediction of sepsis with gradient boosting and deep learning

Feb 2022 to Jun 2022. Bachelor's thesis, Universidad de Sevilla.

Sepsis is a life-threatening response to infection, and each hour of delayed treatment raises mortality. The project predicts its onset in intensive care patients from static and time-series clinical data (MIMIC-III), with a prediction horizon of up to 7 hours.

I trained tree-based models and recurrent networks (LSTM and GRU) with nested cross-validation, and tuned them with Bayesian optimization and a custom weighted AUPRC metric. The 7-hour model reached 0.575 AUPRC (state of the art: 0.4). The 1-hour model reached 0.9 AUC-ROC (state of the art: 0.92).

XGBoost, LSTM, GRU, scikit-learn, MIMIC-III
